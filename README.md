HealthConnect — Week 8: Final Analytics & Decision Support

Overview

Week 8 focused on bringing together the analytical work completed throughout the HealthConnect project into a final decision-support output.

The analysis focused on understanding appointment no-shows, validating key findings, refining the analysis, and translating the results into practical recommendations.

Key Findings

* Overall no-show rate: 48.46%
* Appointments booked 31+ days ahead had a 60.49% no-show rate.
* Patients with 2+ previous no-shows had a 61.02% no-show rate.
* The highest observed risk was among patients with 2+ previous no-shows who booked 31+ days ahead, with a 72.96% no-show rate.
* Appointments with reminders had a 47.36% no-show rate compared with 51.39% without reminders.

Testing & Refinement

The final stage involved:

* Revalidating important dashboard figures.
* Testing key attendance and no-show patterns.
* Refining the analysis to focus on meaningful and actionable patterns.
* Reviewing the predictive model results.
* Translating analytical findings into business recommendations.

Recommendations

* Prioritize follow-up for higher-risk appointment groups.
* Strengthen reminders, particularly for appointments booked far in advance.
* Monitor previous attendance behaviour when planning appointment follow-up.
* Continue tracking key no-show indicators to support ongoing decision-making.

Limitations

The available dataset did not contain enough information about the reasons behind missed appointments. Factors such as transportation challenges, emergencies, work schedules, or changes in personal circumstances were not captured.

Future analysis with richer patient and appointment information could provide deeper insight into why no-shows occur.

Final Outcome

The final analytics output moves HealthConnect beyond simply reporting missed appointments by identifying specific patterns that can support targeted appointment management and follow-up.
